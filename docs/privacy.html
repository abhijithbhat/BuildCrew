Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026 • BuildCrew Android Application (Package: com.abhijithbhat.buildcrew)

This Privacy Policy explains how BuildCrew (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the BuildCrew mobile application (Android package com.abhijithbhat.buildcrew) and related backend services.

1. Purpose of the Application

The sole purpose of BuildCrew is to provide a collaborative project contribution ledger and verified developer passport for software engineering teams. We collect only the information necessary to record, verify, and present project milestones and individual engineering contributions.

2. Data We Collect

When you use BuildCrew, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Identity Data: Your email address, display name, account IDs, and, when signing in with GitHub or Google, your GitHub username and profile avatar URL.
  • Project & Team Details: Project names, team names, and team role declarations.
  • Contribution Entries: Titles, descriptions, contribution categories, dates, evidence links, and uploaded evidence files (such as screenshots, diagrams, and documentation).
  • Peer Review & Governance Actions: Milestone confirmation and dispute actions submitted by team members.
  • Connected Repository Metadata: For teams that choose to connect a repository through the BuildCrew GitHub App, commit metadata, pull-request metadata, and issue metadata originating from that repository.

3. Third-Party Service Processors

We work with trusted third-party service providers to power core functionality of BuildCrew:

  • Supabase: Provides user authentication services, database management, and encrypted file storage for uploaded evidence.
  • Resend: Delivers account email verification and password-reset OTP emails.
  • Google & GitHub: Provide federated authentication and single sign-on (SSO).
  • Railway: Hosts our backend application programming interface (API) and application server.
  • GitHub: Synchronizes repository commit, pull-request, and issue metadata through the official BuildCrew GitHub App.

4. Data Sharing & Public Visibility

We believe in developer control and data privacy:

  • Public Passport: Only published and confirmed contributions appear on your public passport page. This public passport page is accessible only via a dedicated link that you explicitly choose to share.
  • Private Team Milestones: Contribution entries that are disputed or marked as needing review are kept private to your team and are never shown publicly.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or project data to any third party.
  • No Third-Party Advertising or Tracking: The BuildCrew app contains no advertising networks, no third-party marketing SDKs, no behavioral analytics tracking, and no external crash-reporting SDKs.

5. Retention & Account Deletion

Your data is kept only for as long as you maintain an active account with BuildCrew.

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account at any time directly within the app by going to Settings → Delete My Account. This permanently removes your account, profile, all your contributions, and all uploaded evidence files.
  • Web or Email Request: If you cannot access the app, you can request permanent deletion following the steps on our Account Deletion page.

6. Security

We protect your information during transmission. All communications between the BuildCrew mobile app, backend servers, and service processors are encrypted using HTTPS in transit.

7. Children’s Privacy

BuildCrew is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.
