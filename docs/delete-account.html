Delete Your Account & Data

Learn how to permanently remove your BuildCrew account, personal information, and project records.

At BuildCrew, we give you full control over your personal data. You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated records at any time through either of the methods outlined below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Method A: In the Mobile App (Instant Deletion)

If you have access to the BuildCrew Android application:

  1. Open the BuildCrew app and log into your account.
  2. Tap the Settings icon in the navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone and select Delete My Account.
  4. Read the confirmation warning and confirm the deletion prompt.

Your account, profile, and associated files will be deleted immediately and permanently.

Method B: Without the Mobile App (Email Request)

If you have uninstalled the app or are unable to access your device, you can request manual deletion via email:

  1. Compose an email from the exact email address registered with your BuildCrew account.
  2. Send the email to: abhijithmbhat@gmail.com
  3. Use the exact subject line: Delete my BuildCrew account

Processing Timeline: Manual email deletion requests are verified and processed within 30 days of receipt. Once verified, all associated data is permanently erased.

What Is Permanently Deleted

When your account deletion is processed, the following items are permanently removed from our active systems and storage:

  • User Account: Authentication credentials, identifiers, and sign-in records.
  • Profile Data: Display name, profile image / avatar URL, and GitHub username link.
  • Project Memberships: Your associations with all teams and project workspaces.
  • Role Declarations: Stored role agreements and project commitments.
  • Contributions: All contribution entries, milestone titles, descriptions, and timestamps submitted under your account.
  • Uploaded Files: All evidence attachments, screenshots, diagrams, and files uploaded to storage.
  • GitHub App Installation: The GitHub App installation is uninstalled for projects where you were the sole member.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted, your contribution records and verified passport cannot be recovered or restored.

Questions or Support

If you encounter any issues during the deletion process, please contact us at abhijithmbhat@gmail.com for assistance.
